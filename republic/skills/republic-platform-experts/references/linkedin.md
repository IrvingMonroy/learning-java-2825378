# LinkedIn — native presentation norms

- Formats: text-first post, document carousel (PDF), native video.
- First 2 lines visible before 'see more' — they carry the whole hook.
- Short paragraphs, one idea each; no hashtags beyond 3; no external links in body.
- Thought-leadership objective most common here; direct response rare and soft.
