# Stage B — Blank-page generation

Purpose: useful starting material when nothing exists. Not finished copy.

Produce, from the Research Brief and Video Vision analysis:
- 3 concepts (one line each, tied to an opportunity in the brief)
- 5 hooks per chosen concept (mix: question, demonstration-first, observation, contrast, story-open)
- 1 outline per concept
- Story directions (real moments from intake, never invented anecdotes)
- Structure options (list, walkthrough, before/after, myth-vs-fact, FAQ)
- 1 rough first draft for the strongest concept

Rules: no CTAs at this stage; no selling language unless the objective is already
DIRECT RESPONSE; label everything `starting-material`.
