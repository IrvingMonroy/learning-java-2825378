# Stage A — Voice DNA

Voice is extracted, never authored. Sources, in priority order:
1. Transcripts of Rachel (or the client) speaking — classes, voice notes, Socrates captures
2. Emails and DMs they wrote
3. Existing published posts they approved
4. Conversations and comment replies
5. Approved writing in `approved-examples/`

Extract and keep updated in `voice.md`:
- **Recurring phrases** (verbatim, with a source each)
- **Preferred vocabulary** and **disliked vocabulary** (words they never use)
- **Cadence**: sentence length, fragments, paragraph rhythm
- **Humor**: type, frequency, where it appears
- **Level of polish**: how finished vs. conversational the writing is
- **Openers and closers** they actually use

When a draft is written, the QA check compares against this file, not against a
generic "good writing" standard.
