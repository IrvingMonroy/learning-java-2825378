# Writing QA checklist — "Would this person actually say this?"

Every draft, every time. Mark each PASS/FAIL with the offending line quoted.

| # | Check | Test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generic AI phrasing | Any line that could open any brand's post? |
| 2 | Fake profundity | Grand statement with no concrete referent? |
| 3 | Excessive polish | More finished than `voice.md → polish level`? |
| 4 | Repetitive rhetorical patterns | Triads, "It's not X, it's Y", rhetorical questions stacked? |
| 5 | Unnecessary summaries | Restates what was just said? |
| 6 | Clichés | Phrases the audience has read a thousand times? |
| 7 | Invented claims | Anything not traceable to proof/offers/brief? |
| 8 | Excessive adjectives | Strip them: does meaning survive? Then strip them. |
| 9 | Voice drift | Compare cadence, vocabulary, humor against `voice.md`. |
| 10 | Forced CTAs | CTA on a BRAND WRITING objective? Any push where the voice invites? |
| 11 | Phrases the client doesn't use | Cross-check `voice.md → disliked`. |

Goal is not to fool a detector. Goal is fidelity to a real person.
Result recorded in draft front matter: `writing_qa: PASS | FAIL [ids]`.
