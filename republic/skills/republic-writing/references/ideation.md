# Ideation / starting material (from Blank Page Killer)
Only when no draft exists. From the research brief and video analysis produce:
3 concepts · 5 hooks per chosen concept (question, demonstration-first, observation, contrast,
story-open) · 1 outline per concept · story directions from real moments only · structure
options (list, walkthrough, before/after, myth-vs-fact, FAQ) · 1 rough draft for the strongest.
No CTAs. No selling language unless the objective is already conversion. Label `starting-material`.
