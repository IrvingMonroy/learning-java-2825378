# Voice DNA (from Ghostwriter Killer)
Voice is extracted, never authored. Sources in priority: transcripts of the person speaking;
emails and DMs they wrote; posts they approved; comment replies; approved examples.
Extract: recurring phrases (verbatim, sourced) · preferred vocabulary · disliked vocabulary ·
cadence (sentence length, fragments, rhythm) · humor (type, frequency) · level of polish ·
openers and closers actually used. Drafts are judged against this, not against "good writing."
