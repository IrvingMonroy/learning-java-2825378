# Final voice QA (from AI Slop Killer) — "Would this person actually say this?"
Every draft, every time. Quote the offending line on any FAIL.
| # | Check |
|---|---|
| 1 | Generic AI phrasing — could this open any brand's post? |
| 2 | Fake profundity — grand statement, no concrete referent |
| 3 | Excessive polish — more finished than the voice profile's polish level |
| 4 | Repetitive rhetorical patterns — triads, "it's not X, it's Y", stacked questions |
| 5 | Unnecessary summaries — restates what was just said |
| 6 | Clichés |
| 7 | Invented claims — anything not traceable to proof/offers/brief |
| 8 | Excessive adjectives — strip them; if meaning survives, keep them stripped |
| 9 | Voice drift — cadence, vocabulary, humor vs the profile |
| 10 | Forced CTAs — any CTA on a brand-writing objective; any push where the voice invites |
| 11 | Phrases the person doesn't use — check disliked vocabulary |
The goal is not to fool a detector. It is fidelity to a real person. Record `voice_qa: PASS | FAIL [ids]`.
